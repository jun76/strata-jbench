**Structure Summary**

The dataset consists of sequential records labeled from `record000001` up to `record006733`. Each record follows a consistent format:

- **Identifier**: `record` followed by a 6-digit zero-padded number (e.g., `000001`).
- **Content**: A fixed string of Greek letters: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.
- **Separator**: A colon (`: `) separates the ID from the content.
- **Delimiter**: Records are separated by newlines.
