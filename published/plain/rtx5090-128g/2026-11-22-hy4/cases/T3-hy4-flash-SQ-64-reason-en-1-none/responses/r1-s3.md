1. **Prize in Box A**  
   - A: "The prize is in this box." → **True**  
   - B: "The prize is not in this box." → **True** (prize is in A, not in B)  
   - C: "The prize is not in box A." → **False**  
   → Two labels true → violates “exactly one true.”

2. **Prize in Box B**  
   - A: "The prize is in this box." → **False**  
   - B: "The prize is not in this box." → **False**  
   - C: "The prize is not in box A." → **True**  
   → Exactly one true → valid.

3. **Prize in Box C**  
   - A: "The prize is in this box." → **False**  
   - B: "The prize is not in this box." → **True**  
   - C: "The prize is not in box A." → **True**  
   → Two labels true → invalid.

Only **Box B** satisfies the condition.
