The records form a simple, uniform numbered list.

- Each line has the format:

  `recordNNNNNN: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`

- The ID is the `record` label followed by a 6-digit zero-padded number, for example:
  - `record000001`
  - `record000999`
  - `record001721`

- Every record has the same content after the colon:

  `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`
