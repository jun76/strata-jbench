The records form a simple, highly regular numbered log.

## Structure

Each line has the format:

```text
record<ID>: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.
```

Where:

- `record` is a fixed prefix.
- `<ID>` is a zero-padded 6-digit decimal number, ranging here from `000001` to `001721`.
- A colon `:` separates the record ID from the payload.
- The payload is always the same eight Greek-letter tokens:
  - `alpha`
  - `beta`
  - `gamma`
  - `delta`
