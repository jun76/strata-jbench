The records form a simple, uniform list:

- Each line is one record.
- Every record has the format:

```text
recordNNNNNN: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.
```

- `record` is a fixed label.
- `NNNNNN` is a 6-digit zero-padded numeric ID, ranging here from `000001` to `001721`.
- After the colon and space, every record contains the same fixed sequence of eight Greek-letter placeholder words:

```text
alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.
```
