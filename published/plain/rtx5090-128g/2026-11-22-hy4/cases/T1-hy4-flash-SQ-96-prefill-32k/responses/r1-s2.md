The records form a simple, highly uniform list:

- Each line has the format:

  `recordNNNNNN: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`

- `NNNNNN` is a zero-padded 6-digit record ID.
  - The first record is `record000001`.
  - The last record shown is `record001721`.
- After the colon, every record contains the same eight Greek-letter tokens:
  1. alpha
  2. beta
  3. gamma
  4. delta
  5. epsilon
  6. zeta
  7. eta
