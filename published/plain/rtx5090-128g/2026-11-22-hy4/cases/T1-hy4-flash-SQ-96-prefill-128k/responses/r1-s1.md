The records form a simple, highly uniform list:

- Each line has the form:

  `recordNNNNNN: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`

- `record` is a fixed prefix.
- `NNNNNN` is a six-digit zero-padded numeric ID.
- After the colon and space, every record contains the same eight Greek-letter tokens in the same order:

  `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`

- The line ends with a period.
- The visible IDs run consecutively from `record000001` through `record006733`.
