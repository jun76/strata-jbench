The records form a simple, uniformly structured list:

- Each line is one record.
- Every record ID has the form `record` followed by a 6-digit zero-padded number, e.g. `record000001`, `record000212`.
- The IDs are sequential and contiguous from `record000001` through `record000212`.
- Every record has the exact same content after the ID:

```text
alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.
```

So the general format is:

```text
recordNNNNNN: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.
```

where `NNNNNN
