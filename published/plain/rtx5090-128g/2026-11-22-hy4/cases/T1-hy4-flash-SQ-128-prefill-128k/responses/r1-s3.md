The records form a simple, highly uniform list:

- Each line is one record.
- Every record has the form:

```text
recordNNNNNN: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.
```

- `NNNNNN` is a zero-padded 6-digit record ID.
- After the colon, every record contains the same fixed token sequence:

```text
alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.
```

- The IDs are consecutive, starting at `record000001` and ending at `record006733`.
- There are no apparent gaps or variations in the content.
