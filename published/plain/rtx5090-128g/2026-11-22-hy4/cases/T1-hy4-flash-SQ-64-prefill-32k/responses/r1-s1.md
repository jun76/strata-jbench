The dataset is a sequence of records numbered consecutively from:

`record000001` through `record001721`

Each record has the same structure:

`record<ID>: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`

So every line contains:

1. A unique record ID, formatted as `record` followed by a 6-digit zero-padded number.
2. A colon separator.
3. A fixed token sequence: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.

There are 1,721 records total, and the content after the colon is identical in every record.
