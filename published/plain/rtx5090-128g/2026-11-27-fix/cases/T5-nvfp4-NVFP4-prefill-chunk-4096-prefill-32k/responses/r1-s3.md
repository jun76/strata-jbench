### 1. Structure of the Records

Each record follows a strict, uniform schema consisting of two main components:

*   **Identifier (ID):**
    *   **Format:** `record` followed by a zero-padded integer.
    *   **Pattern:** `record000001`, `record000002`, etc.
    *   **Length:** The numeric part is padded with leading zeros to ensure it is always **6 digits long**.
