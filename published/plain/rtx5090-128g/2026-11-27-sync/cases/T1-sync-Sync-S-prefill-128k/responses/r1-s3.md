The records follow a uniform, highly repetitive structure. Each line contains:

1. **A unique identifier** formatted as `record` followed by a zero-padded seven-digit number (e.g., `record000001`, `record001234`, `record006733`).
2. **A colon** separating the ID from the content.
3. **Identical content** consisting of the Greek letters in order: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.
