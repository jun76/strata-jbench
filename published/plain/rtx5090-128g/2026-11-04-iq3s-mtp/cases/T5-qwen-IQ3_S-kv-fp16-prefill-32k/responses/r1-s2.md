### 1. Structure Analysis

The dataset consists of a linear sequence of text records. Each record follows a strict, uniform format:

*   **Format:** `record[8-digit ID]: [Greek Letter Sequence].`
*   **Prefix:** The word `record` followed immediately by an 8-digit zero-padded integer (e.g., `00000001`, `00000002`).
*   **Separator:** A colon and a space (`: `).
