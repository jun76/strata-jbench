### 1. Structure of the Records

Each line in the dataset represents a single record with a strictly defined format:

**Format:**
`record[6-digit zero-padded integer]: [fixed string]`

**Breakdown:**
*   **Key/ID:** `record` followed by a 6-digit number (e.g., `000001`, `000212`). This acts as a unique identifier.
*   **Delimiter:** A colon and a space
