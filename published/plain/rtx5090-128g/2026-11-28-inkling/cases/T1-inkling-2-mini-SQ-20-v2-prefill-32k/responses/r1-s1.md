The records in this dataset follow a highly uniform, repetitive structure. Each record consists of a unique identifier followed by an identical sequence of eight Greek-letter terms. 

**Structure of a record:**
*   **Identifier:** A sequential alphanumeric code starting with "record" (e.g., record000001, record000002, etc.).
*   **Data Content:** The exact same string of words: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.
