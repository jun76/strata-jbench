**Structure of the Records:**
Each record consists of a unique identifier (`record` followed by a 6-digit number, e.g., `record000001`) followed by a colon and a sequence of eight lowercase Greek letters separated by spaces: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.
