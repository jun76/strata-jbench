The dataset is a simple, uniform list of records.

## Structure

Each line has the same format:

```text
recordNNNNNN: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.
```

- **ID prefix:** `record`
- **Numeric ID:** six digits, e.g. `000001`, `001721`
- **Separator:** a colon `:` followed by a space
- **Payload:** the same eight Greek-letter tokens every time:
  - `alpha`
  - `beta`
  - `gamma`
  - `delta`
  - `epsilon`
  - `zeta`
  - `
