### 1. Structure of the Records

Each record follows a strict, uniform format composed of three distinct parts:

*   **Unique Identifier (Key):**
    *   Format: `record` followed by a 6-digit zero-padded integer (e.g., `record000001`, `record000212`).
    *   This serves as the primary key for the dataset.
*   **Separator:**
    *   A colon `:` followed by a space.
