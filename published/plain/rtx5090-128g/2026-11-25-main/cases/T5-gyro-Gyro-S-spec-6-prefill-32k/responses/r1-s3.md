### Structure Summary

The data consists of a sequential list of records, each identified by a unique **Record ID** and containing identical **content**.

1.  **ID Format**:
    *   Each record ID follows the pattern: `record` + a **7-digit zero-padded integer**.
    *   Example: `record000001`, `record001234`, `record001721`.
    *   The IDs range from `record000001` to `record001721` in this snippet.
