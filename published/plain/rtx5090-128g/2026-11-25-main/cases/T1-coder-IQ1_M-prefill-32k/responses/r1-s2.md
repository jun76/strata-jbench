The records follow a consistent, predictable structure that can be described as follows:

**Structure:**

1.  **Identifier:** Each line begins with a unique ID in the format `recordNNNNNN:` (where `NNNNNN` is a zero-padded 6-digit integer).
2.  **Payload:** After the identifier and colon, every record contains the exact same sequence of 8 Greek letters: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`
