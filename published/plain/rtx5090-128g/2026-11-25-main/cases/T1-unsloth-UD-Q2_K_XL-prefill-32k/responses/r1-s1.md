### 1. Structure of the Records

Each record follows a strict, uniform format consisting of two parts separated by a colon and a space:

**Format:**
`record[ID]: [Content]`

*   **The Key (ID):**
    *   Starts with the literal string `record`.
    *   Followed by a **zero-padded integer** (e.g., `000001`, `000100`, `001721`).
