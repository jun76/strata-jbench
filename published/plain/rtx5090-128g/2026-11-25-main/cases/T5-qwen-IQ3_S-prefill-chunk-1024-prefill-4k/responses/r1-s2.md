### 1. Structure Analysis

Each line in the dataset follows a strict, consistent pattern known as a **Key-Value Pair** or **Record Line**.

**Format:**
`[Key]: [Value]`

**Breakdown:**
*   **Key (Record ID):** Starts with the prefix `record` followed by a zero-padded 6-digit integer (e.g., `record000001`, `record000100`).
*   **Separator:** A colon
