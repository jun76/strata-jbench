A few things worth knowing while you explore:

- **Camera** — drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right‑drag to pan. It auto‑rotates until you touch it.
- **Click the scene** — hit the **lighthouse** to flip day↔night, the **dock bell** to ring it, or the **ship** to send it out to sea (or call it home).
- **Buttons** do the same, plus *Calm* hides the interface for a clean view.
- Everything is real geometry: the harbor is a procedurally sculpted voxel coastline, the sea is a live vertex‑colored wave mesh with matched fog, and the ship is a hand‑built blocky sloop that bobs on the swell.
