The records provided are structurally uniform and contain no varying data. Each record consists of a unique identifier (e.g., `record000001`) followed by an identical sequence of eight lowercase Greek letters: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`. 

To look up a record by its ID:
1. Locate the specific record identifier (e.g., `record000001`) in the list.
2. Read the text that follows the identifier. In this specific dataset, that text will always be the exact same sequence of the eight Greek letters.
