We are asked: Among integers from 1 to 2026 inclusive, how many are **not divisible by 3 nor by 5**?

That is, we want the count of numbers in [1, 2026] that are **not divisible by 3** and **not divisible by 5**.

This is a classic inclusion-exclusion principle problem.

Let:
- Total numbers: \( N = 2026 \)
- A = set of numbers ≤ 2026 divisible by 3
- B = set of numbers ≤ 2026 divisible by 5
- We want \( N - |A \cup B| \)

By inclusion-exclusion:
\[
|A \cup B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cap B|
\]

Where:
- \( |A| = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{3} \right\rfloor \)
- \( |B| = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{5} \right\rfloor \)
- \( |A \cap B| = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{15} \right\rfloor \) (since divisible by both 3 and 5 ⇔ divisible by lcm(3,5)=15)

Compute each:

\[
\left\lfloor \frac{2026}{3} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor 675.333... \right\rfloor = 675
\]

\[
\left\lfloor \frac{2026}{5} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor 405.2 \right\rfloor = 405
\]

\[
\left\lfloor \frac{2026}{15} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor 135.066... \right\rfloor = 135
\]

So:
\[
|A \cup B| = 675 + 405 - 135 = 945
\]

Therefore, the number of integers **not divisible by 3 or 5** is:
\[
2026 - 945 = 1081
\]

Answer: 1081
