We need to count how many integers from 1 to 2026 are **not divisible by 3 and not divisible by 5**.

Let $ N = 2026 $.

We want to count:
$$
\text{Count} = N - |\text{multiples of 3} \cup \text{multiples of 5}|
$$

By inclusion-exclusion:
$$
|A \cup B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cap B|
$$
where:
- $ A $ = multiples of 3
- $ B $ = multiples of 5
- $ A \cap B $ = multiples of 15

Compute each:

1. Multiples of 3 up to 2026:
$$
\left\lfloor \frac{2026}{3} \right\rfloor = 675 \quad (\text{since } 3 \times 675 = 2025)
$$

2. Multiples of 5:
$$
\left\lfloor \frac{2026}{5} \right\rfloor = 405 \quad (\text{since } 5 \times 405 = 2025)
$$

3. Multiples of 15:
$$
\left\lfloor \frac{2026}{15} \right\rfloor = 135 \quad (\text{since } 15 \times 135 = 2025)
$$

So:
$$
|A \cup B| = 675 + 405 - 135 = 945
$$

Therefore, numbers **not divisible by 3 or 5**:
$$
2026 - 945 = 1081
$$

**Answer: 1081**
