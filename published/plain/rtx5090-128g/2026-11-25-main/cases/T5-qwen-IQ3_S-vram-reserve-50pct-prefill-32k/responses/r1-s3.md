### 1. Structure of the Records

Each record follows a strict, repetitive pattern consisting of two parts:

*   **The Identifier (Key):**
    *   Format: `recordNNNNNN`
    *   `record`: A static prefix.
    *   `NNNNNN`: A **6-digit zero-padded integer**. This represents the sequential ID of the record, ranging from `000001` to `001721` in this dataset.
