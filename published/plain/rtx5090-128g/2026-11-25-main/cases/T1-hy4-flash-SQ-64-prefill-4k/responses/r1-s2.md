The records are structured as a simple numbered list:

- Each line has the form: `recordNNNNNN: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`
- The ID is a zero-padded 6-digit number, from `record000001` through `record000212`.
- Every record has the same content: the eight Greek-letter-style tokens  
  `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.
- The records appear in ascending numeric order by ID.
