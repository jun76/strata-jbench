### 1. Structure of the Records

Each record follows a strict, uniform format consisting of two main components:

1.  **Record ID**: A unique identifier in the format `recordXXXXXX`, where `XXXXXX` is a zero-padded integer (6 digits). The IDs start at `000001` and increase sequentially by 1.
2.  **Content**: A fixed string of eight Greek letters separated by spaces: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.
