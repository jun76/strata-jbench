### Structure of the Records

The data consists of a sequential log of 212 records, characterized by a highly repetitive and uniform format.

**1. Format Pattern:**
Each record follows this exact syntax:
`record[6-digit-zero-padded-integer]: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`

**2. Components:**
*   **Key/ID:** `record` followed by a sequential number padded with zeros to 6 digits (e.g., `000001`, `000212`).
*   **Delimiter:** A colon and a space (`:
