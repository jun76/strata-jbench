### 1. Structure of the Records

Each record follows a strict, uniform text format consisting of three components separated by spaces:

`[ID] : [Content]`

*   **ID Field:**
    *   Starts with the literal string `record`.
    *   Followed by a **7-digit zero-padded integer** (e.g., `0000001`, `001721`).
    *   The ID acts as the unique key for the record.
