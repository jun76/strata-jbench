The records form a simple, highly uniform list.

## Structure

Each line has this format:

```text
recordNNNNNN: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.
```

- **ID prefix:** `record`
- **Numeric ID:** a 6-digit zero-padded number, from `000001` through `001721`
- **Separator:** a colon followed by a space (`: `)
- **Payload:** the same eight Greek-letter tokens on every record:

```text
alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.
```

So every record has identical content except for its ID.
