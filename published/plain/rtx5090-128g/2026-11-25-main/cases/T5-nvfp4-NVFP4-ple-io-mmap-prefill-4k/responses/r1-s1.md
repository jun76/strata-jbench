### 1. Structure of the Records

Each record follows a strict, uniform format consisting of two components:

*   **Key (Identifier):** A string in the format `recordXXXXXX`, where `XXXXXX` is a zero-padded integer representing the sequential ID.
    *   Example: `record000001`, `record000002`, ..., `record000212`.
    *   The padding ensures lexicographical sorting matches numerical sorting.
