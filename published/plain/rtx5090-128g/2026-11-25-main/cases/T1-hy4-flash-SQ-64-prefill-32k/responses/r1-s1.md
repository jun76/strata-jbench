The records form a simple, highly uniform numbered list.

Structure:

- Each line has the form:

  `recordNNNNNN: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`

- The ID is a zero-padded 6-digit number after `record`.
  - Examples: `record000001`, `record000020`, `record001000`, `record001721`.
- After the colon, every record contains the same eight Greek-letter tokens in the same order:
  - `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`
- The records are consecutive from `record000001` through `record001721
