To solve this problem, we need to count the number of integers from 1 to 2026 that are **not divisible by 3** and **not divisible by 5**. This is equivalent to finding the complement of the set of numbers divisible by 3 or 5.

We can use the Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion.

1.  **Total count of integers:**
    $$N = 2026$$

2.  **Count of numbers divisible by 3:**
    $$\left\lfloor \frac{2026}{3} \right\rfloor = 675$$

3.  **Count of numbers divisible by 5:**
    $$\left\lfloor \frac{2026}{5} \right\rfloor = 405$$

4.  **Count of numbers divisible by both 3 and 5 (i.e., divisible by 15):**
    $$\left\lfloor \frac{2026}{15} \right\rfloor = 135$$

5.  **Count of numbers divisible by 3 OR 5:**
    Using the inclusion-exclusion principle:
    $$N_{\text{div by 3 or 5}} = 675 + 405 - 135 = 945$$

6.  **Count of numbers NOT divisible by 3 or 5:**
    $$N_{\text{valid}} = \text{Total} - N_{\text{div by 3 or 5}}$$
    $$N_{\text{valid}} = 2026 - 945 = 1081$$

Alternatively, using the periodicity method:
In every block of 15 consecutive integers, there are $15 - 7 = 8$ integers not divisible by 3 or 5.
$$2026 = 135 \times 15 + 1$$
There are 135 full blocks contributing $135 \times 8 = 1080$ valid numbers.
The remaining number is 2026. Since 2026 is not divisible by 3 (digit sum 10) nor by 5 (ends in 6), it is also a valid number.
$$1080 + 1 = 1081$$

The result is 1081.
