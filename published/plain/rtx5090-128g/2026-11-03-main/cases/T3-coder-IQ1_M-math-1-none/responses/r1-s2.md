We need to count how many integers from 1 to 2026 are **not divisible by 3 and not divisible by 5**.

Let $N = 2026$.

We want to count:
$$
\text{Count} = N - |\text{multiples of 3} \cup \text{multiples of 5}|
$$

By inclusion-exclusion:
$$
|A \cup B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cap B|
$$
where:
- $A$: multiples of 3 in [1, 2026]
- $B$: multiples of 5 in [1, 2026]
- $A \cap B$: multiples of 15 in [1, 2026]

Compute:
- $|A| = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{3} \right\rfloor = 675$
- $|B| = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{5} \right\rfloor = 405$
- $|A \cap B| = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{15} \right\rfloor = 135$

So:
$$
|A \cup B| = 675 + 405 - 135 = 945
$$

Therefore:
$$
\text{Answer} = 2026 - 945 = 1081
$$

Alternatively, we can use Euler's phi-like counting or periodicity modulo 15. In every block of 15 consecutive integers, the numbers not divisible by 3 or 5 are those coprime to 15: 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 11, 13, 14 → 8 numbers per 15.

Number of full blocks of 15 in 2026: 2026 ÷ 15 = 135 full blocks (since 15×135 = 2025), remainder 1.

Full blocks: 135 × 8 = 1080
Remainder: number 2026 = 15×135 + 1 → corresponds to residue 1 mod 15 → which is coprime to 15 → +1

Total = 1080 + 1 = 1081. Confirmed.
