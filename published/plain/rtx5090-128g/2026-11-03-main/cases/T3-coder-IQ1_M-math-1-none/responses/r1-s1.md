要解决这个问题，我们需要找出如何 many integers between 1 and 2026 are **not divisible by 3** and **not divisible by 5**.

Let $N = 2026$.
Let $A$ be the set of integers in $[1, N]$ divisible by 3.
Let $B$ be the set of integers in $[1, N]$ divisible by 5.

We want to find the number of integers not in $A \cup B$.
This is equal to the total number of integers ($N$) minus the size of the union ($|A \cup B|$).

According to the Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion:
$$ |A \cup B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cap B| $$

**Step 1: Calculate $|A|$ (numbers divisible by 3)**
$$ |A| = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{3} \right\rfloor $$
$$ 2026 \div 3 = 675.333... $$
$$ |A| = 675 $$

**Step 2: Calculate $|B|$ (numbers divisible by 5)**
$$ |B| = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{5} \right\rfloor $$
$$ 2026 \div 5 = 405.2 $$
$$ |B| = 405 $$

**Step 3: Calculate $|A \cap B|$ (numbers divisible by both 3 and 5)**
A number is divisible by both 3 and 5 if and only if it is divisible by their least common multiple, which is $\text{lcm}(3, 5) = 15$.
$$ |A \cap B| = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{15} \right\rfloor $$
$$ 2026 \div 15 = 135.066... $$
$$ |A \cap B| = 135 $$

**Step 4: Calculate $|A \cup B|$**
$$ |A \cup B| = 675 + 405 - 135 $$
$$ |A \cup B| = 1080 - 135 = 945 $$

**Step 5: Calculate the number of integers NOT divisible by 3 or 5**
$$ \text{Count} = N - |A \cup B| $$
$$ \text{Count} = 2026 - 945 $$
$$ \text{Count} = 1081 $$

Alternatively, we can use the Euler's totient function logic for blocks of 15:
In every block of 15 consecutive integers, the numbers coprime to 15 (i.e., not divisible by 3 or 5) are those with remainders 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 11, 13, 14 when divided by 15. There are 8 such numbers in each block of 15.
$$ 2026 = 15 \times 135 + 1 $$
So there are 135 full blocks of 15.
$$ 135 \times 8 = 1080 $$
The remaining number is the 1st number in the next block, which is $15 \times 135 + 1 = 2026$.
Is 2026 divisible by 3 or 5?
$2026$ ends in 6, so not divisible by 5.
Sum of digits: $2+0+2+6=10$, not divisible by 3.
So 2026 is not divisible by 3 or 5. It counts.
Total = $1080 + 1 = 1081$.

Both methods yield 1081.

答え: 1081
