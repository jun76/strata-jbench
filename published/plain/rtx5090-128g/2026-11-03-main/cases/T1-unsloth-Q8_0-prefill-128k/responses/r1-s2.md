### 1. Structure of the Records

The dataset consists of sequential text entries with a highly repetitive and uniform structure.

*   **Format:** `record[7-digit-ID]: [Content]`
*   **ID Component:**
    *   Starts with the static string `record`.
    *   Followed by a zero-padded 7-digit integer (starting from `0000001` and incrementing by 1).
    *   Ends with a colon and a space `: `.
