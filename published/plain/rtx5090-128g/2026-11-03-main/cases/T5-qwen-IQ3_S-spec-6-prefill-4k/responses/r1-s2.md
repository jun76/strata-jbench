Based on the data provided, here is an analysis of the record structure and the method for retrieving specific records.

### 1. Structure of the Records

Each line in the dataset follows a strict, uniform format consisting of two main parts separated by a colon (`:`):

*   **Identifier (Key):** `recordXXXXXX`
    *   This is a string starting with the prefix `record`.
    *   Followed by a **6-digit zero-padded integer** (e.g., `000001`, `000100`, `000212`).
