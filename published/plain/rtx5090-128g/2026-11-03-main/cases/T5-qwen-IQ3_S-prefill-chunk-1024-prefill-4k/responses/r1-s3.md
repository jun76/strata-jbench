### 1. Structure of the Records

The dataset consists of sequential log entries with a consistent, simple format.

**Format Pattern:**
`record<ID>: <content>`

**Components:**
*   **ID (`record<ID>`):** A unique identifier consisting of the literal string `record` followed by a zero-padded 6-digit integer.
    *   *Example:* `record000001`, `record000212`.
    *   The IDs are strictly sequential
