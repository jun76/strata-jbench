TUMBLEGRASS

A hand-generated world of rolling hills, pine forests and lakes. Dig it out, stack it up, and watch the sun crawl across the sky.

WASD move around
SPACE jump
SHIFT toggle fly
MOUSE look & aim
L-CLICK place block
R-CLICK break block
1–9 choose block
WHEEL cycle blocks
Tip: left-click to place, right-click to break. Press Esc to release the mouse.
