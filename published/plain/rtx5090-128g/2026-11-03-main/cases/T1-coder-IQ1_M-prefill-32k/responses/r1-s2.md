The records follow a consistent, predictable structure that can be described as follows:

**Structure:**

1.  **Identifier:** Each line begins with a unique ID in the format `recordNNNNNN:` (where `NNNNNN` is a zero-padded 6-digit integer, starting from `000001` and incrementing sequentially).
2.  **Payload:** Following the colon and a space, every record contains the exact same sequence of eight lowercase Greek letter names: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.
