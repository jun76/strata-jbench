### 1. Structure of the Records

Each record follows a strict, uniform format consisting of three components:

*   **Identifier (Key):** A string starting with `record` followed by a zero-padded 6-digit integer (e.g., `record000001`).
*   **Separator:** A colon and a space (`: `).
*   **Payload (Value):** A fixed sequence of eight lowercase Greek letters, separated by spaces: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.
