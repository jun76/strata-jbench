The provided dataset consists of a sequential list of text records. Here is a summary of the structure and how to look up a specific record.

### 1. Structure of the Records

Each record follows a strict, uniform format consisting of two parts separated by a colon:

*   **ID**: A fixed-width identifier in the format `recordXXXXXX`.
    *   The prefix is always `record`.
    *   The number is padded with leading zeros to ensure it is always 6 digits long (e.g., `record000001`, `record001000`).
