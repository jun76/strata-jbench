### Structure of the Records

The dataset consists of **1,721** sequential records (`record000001` to `record001721`). Each record follows a strict, uniform format:

**Format:**
`recordXXXXXX: alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`

**Components:**
1.  **ID:** A fixed-width string `record` followed by a 6-digit number (zero-padded).
