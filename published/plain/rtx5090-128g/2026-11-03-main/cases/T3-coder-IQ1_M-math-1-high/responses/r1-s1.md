1から2026中2026個整数中、3或5 divisible numbers are counted using the inclusion-exclusion principle.

\[
\left\lfloor\frac{2026}{3}\right\rfloor=675,\quad
\left\lfloor\frac{2026}{5}\right\rfloor=405,\quad
\left\lfloor\frac{2026}{15}\right\rfloor=135
\]

Therefore,

\[
2026-(675+405-135)=2026-945=1081
\]

答え: 1081
