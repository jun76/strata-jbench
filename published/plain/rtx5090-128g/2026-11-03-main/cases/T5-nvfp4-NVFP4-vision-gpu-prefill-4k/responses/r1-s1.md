Based on the data provided, here is an analysis of the record structure and the method for retrieving specific records.

### 1. Structure of the Records

The records follow a strict, uniform pattern consisting of two components:

*   **Identifier (Key):** A string formatted as `record` followed by a **6-digit zero-padded integer**.
    *   Format: `recordNNNNNN`
    *   Range in this dataset: `000001` to `000212`.
*   **Content (Value):** A fixed, static sequence of eight Greek letters.
