### 1. Structure of the Records

Each line in the dataset follows a strict, uniform format composed of two main parts:

*   **The Identifier (Key):**
    *   Format: `record` followed by a **6-digit zero-padded integer**.
    *   Example: `record000001`, `record000123`, `record000212`.
    *   The identifier is separated from the data by a colon and a space
