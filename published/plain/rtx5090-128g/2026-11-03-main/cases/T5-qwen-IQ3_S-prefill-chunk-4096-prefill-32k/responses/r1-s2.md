### 1. Structure of the Records

The dataset consists of sequential log entries with a highly repetitive and uniform format. Each line follows this specific syntax:

**`record[7-digit ID]: [Content]`**

*   **Prefix:** The string `record` is constant for every entry.
*   **ID:** A **7-digit zero-padded integer** immediately follows the prefix.
    *   The IDs start at `0000001` and increment by 1
