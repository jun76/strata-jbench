Here is a summary of the structure of the records provided and instructions on how to look up a specific record by its ID.

### 1. Structure Summary
*   **Format:** Each record follows a consistent pattern: `record000XXX: [Content]`.
*   **Content:** All records (from `record000001` to `record000212`) contain the exact same string of Greek letters: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`
*   **ID Pattern:** The IDs are sequential integers starting from 1 up to 212.
