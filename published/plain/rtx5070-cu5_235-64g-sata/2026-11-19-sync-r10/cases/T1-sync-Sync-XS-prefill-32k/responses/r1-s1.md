### Summary of Structure

The dataset consists of **sequential, fixed-format records** numbered from `record000001` to `record001721`.

**Key Characteristics:**
*   **Uniform Payload:** Every record contains the identical string: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.
*   **Sequential IDs:** IDs are zero-padded to 7 digits (e.g., `000001`), ensuring lexicographical sorting matches numerical sorting.
*   **Format:** `record[7-digit-ID]: [content]`.
