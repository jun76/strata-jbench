The records follow a uniform, highly repetitive structure. Each line contains:

- **Record ID**: `recordXXXXXXX` (zero-padded to 7 digits), ranging from `record000001` to `record006733`.
- **Payload**: The same fixed string: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`

To look up a record by its ID:

1. Locate the line that begins with the exact ID (e.g., `record001234:`).
2. Read the text after the colon and space — that is the record
