### Structure of the Records

The dataset consists of a linear sequence of records, each representing a line of text. The structure is uniform across all entries:

1.  **Format**: Each record follows the pattern `recordXXXXXX: content`.
2.  **ID Field**: The identifier is `recordXXXXXX`, where `XXXXXX` is a zero-padded integer ranging from `000001` to `001721`.
3.  **Content Field**: The content is identical for every record: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`
