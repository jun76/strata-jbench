- $S_A$: "The prize is in this box." (Prize is in A)

- Therefore, the prize is not in Box A.

- $S_C$ ("Prize is not in A") is **True** (prize is in B, so it is indeed not in A).
- Result: Exactly one label ($S_C$) is true. This matches the condition.
- Therefore, this is a valid solution.
