### 1. Structure of the Records

Each record in this dataset follows a strict, consistent format consisting of two main components separated by a colon (`:`):

*   **Key (Record ID):**
    *   Format: `record` followed by a zero-padded integer.
    *   Example: `record000001`, `record000050`, `record000212`.
    *   The ID starts at `000001`
