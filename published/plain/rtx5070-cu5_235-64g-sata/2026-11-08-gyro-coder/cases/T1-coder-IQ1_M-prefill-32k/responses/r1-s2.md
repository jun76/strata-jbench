The records follow a simple, uniform structure. Here is the breakdown:

### Structure
Each line in the dataset represents a single record with two components:
1.  **Identifier (ID):** A unique, zero-padded number starting with `record` (e.g., `record000001`, `record000002`).
2.  **Payload (Data):** A static string of Greek letters that is identical for every record: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.
