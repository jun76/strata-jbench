要 solve this problem, we need to find the number of integers from 1 to 2026 that are **not** divisible by 3 and **not** divisible by 5.

Let $S$ be the set of integers from 1 to 2026. The total number of elements is $|S| = 2026$.

Let $A$ be the set of integers in $S$ divisible by 3.
Let $B$ be the set of integers in $S$ divisible by 5.

We want to find the number of elements that are not in $A$ and not in $B$. This is the complement of the union of $A$ and $B$ within $S$.
According to the Inclusion-Exclusion Principle, the size of the union $|A \cup B|$ is:
$$ |A \cup B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cap B| $$

The number of integers not divisible by 3 or 5 is:
$$ |S| - |A \cup B| $$

**Step 1: Calculate $|A|$ (divisible by 3)**
$$ |A| = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{3} \right\rfloor $$
$$ 2026 \div 3 = 675.333\dots \implies |A| = 675 $$

**Step 2: Calculate $|B|$ (divisible by 5)**
$$ |B| = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{5} \right\rfloor $$
$$ 2026 \div 5 = 405.2 \implies |B| = 405 $$

**Step 3: Calculate $|A \cap B|$ (divisible by both 3 and 5, i.e., divisible by 15)**
The least common multiple of 3 and 5 is 15.
$$ |A \cap B| = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{15} \right\rfloor $$
$$ 2026 \div 15 = 135.066\dots \implies |A \cap B| = 135 $$

**Step 4: Calculate $|A \cup B|$ (divisible by 3 or 5)**
$$ |A \cup B| = 675 + 405 - 135 $$
$$ |A \cup B| = 1080 - 135 = 945 $$

**Step 5: Calculate the final answer (not divisible by 3 or 5)**
$$ \text{Result} = 2026 - 945 $$
$$ \text{Result} = 1081 $$

Alternatively, using the density method for verification:
In every group of 15 consecutive integers, there are 8 numbers not divisible by 3 or 5 (since $\phi(15) = 15(1 - 1/3)(1 - 1/5) = 8$).
$2026 = 15 \times 135 + 1$.
There are 135 full groups of 15, contributing $135 \times 8 = 1080$ numbers.
The remaining 1 number is 2026 (or the first number in the partial block depending on offset, but let's check the remainder).
Actually, let's just check the remainder directly.
Numbers from $15 \times 135 + 1 = 2026$ to $2026$. Only one number: 2026.
Is 2026 divisible by 3? Sum of digits $2+0+2+6=10$, no.
Is 2026 divisible by 5? Ends in 6, no.
So 2026 is counted.
Total = $1080 + 1 = 1081$.

Both methods yield 1081.

答案: 1081
