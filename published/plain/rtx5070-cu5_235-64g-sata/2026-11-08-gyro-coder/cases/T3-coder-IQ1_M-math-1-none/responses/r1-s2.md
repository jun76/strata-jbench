We need to count how many integers from 1 to 2026 are **not divisible by 3** and **not divisible by 5**.

Let $N = 2026$.

We want:
$$
\text{Count} = N - \#\{x \in [1,N] : 3 \mid x \text{ or } 5 \mid x\}
$$

By inclusion-exclusion:
$$
\#\{3|x \text{ or } 5|x\} = \#\{3|x\} + \#\{5|x\} - \#\{15|x\}
$$

Compute:
- $\#\{3|x\} = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{3} \right\rfloor$
- $\#\{5|x\} = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{5} \right\rfloor$
- $\#\{15|x\} = \left\lfloor \frac{2026}{15} \right\rfloor$

Now compute:

$2026 \div 3 = 675.333... \Rightarrow \lfloor 2026/3 \rfloor = 675$

$2026 \div 5 = 405.2 \Rightarrow \lfloor 2026/5 \rfloor = 405$

$2026 \div 15 = 135.066... \Rightarrow \lfloor 2026/15 \rfloor = 135$

So:
$$
\#\{3|x \text{ or } 5|x\} = 675 + 405 - 135 = 945
$$

Thus, numbers **not divisible by 3 or 5**:
$$
2026 - 945 = 1081
$$
