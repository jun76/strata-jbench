3 再 5 再 再 multiples ofの count:

- Multiples of 3: ⌊2026 / 3⌋ = 675  
- Multiples of 5: ⌊2026 / 5⌋ = 405  
- Multiples of 15（double-counted）: ⌊2026 / 15⌋ = 135  

Thereby, multiples of 3 or 5:

675 + 405 − 135 = 945

Not divisible by 3 and not divisible by 5 count:

2026 − 945 = 1081
