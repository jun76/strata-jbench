日本は四季節の国で、四季の变化は深刻に影響人们的生活、食文化、年中行事。以下详细説明。

**1. 春（3月-5月）： renewal and celebration**

春は日本の season of renewal. After winter, people feel hope and joy. Spring brings new life and new traditions.

**Lifestyle:**
In spring, people start to enjoy outdoor activities. For example, cherry blossom viewing (sakura viewing) is a major activity. People go to parks like Yoyama Park in Tokyo or the cherry blossom avenue in Kyoto. They bring picnic mats and eat snacks. This is a social event. Families and friends gather. It is a time for relaxation and community.

Also, spring is a time for cleaning. After winter, people clean their houses. They open windows to let in fresh air. They wash clothes and curtains. This is a practical lifestyle change.

**Food Culture:**
Spring foods are fresh and light. People eat seasonal vegetables. For example, asparagus (asparagus) and bamboo shoots (take) are popular. Take is a young bamboo shoot. It is bitter but delicious. People cook it in soy sauce or make it into a pickle.

Seafood is also important. Spring brings new fish. For example, shad (ginkgo? no, shad is not common. Let's use correct fish: sea bream or cod). Actually, in spring, people eat young vegetables and fresh seafood. For example, oysters are harvested in spring. Also, spring onions (scallions) are used in many dishes.

A famous spring food is "spring roll" (haru roll). It is a sushi roll with fresh vegetables. It is light and refreshing. People eat it in restaurants.

Another example is "cherry blossom tea". People drink tea with cherry blossom flavor. It is a special drink for spring.

**Year-end Events (Spring Events):**
Spring has many festivals. The most famous is the cherry blossom festival. In Tokyo, the "Sakura Matsuri" is held. People enjoy the beauty of flowers.
