日本は四季節の国，その季節性变化は深刻影響人们的生活、食文化、年中行事。以下详细説明。

**1. 春（3月～5月）： renewal and celebration**

*   **Lifestyle:** Spring is the season of renewal. People start gardening, planting flowers like tulips and daffodils. Homes are cleaned, and windows are opened to let in fresh air. The feeling of "new beginning" is strong.
*   **Food Culture:** Spring brings fresh ingredients. Asparagus, bamboo shoots (taken from forests), and wild vegetables like fuki (coltsfoot) are harvested. Seafood like shiobashira (salted young shad) is popular. Desserts like sakura-flavored sweets (sakura mochi, sakura ice cream) are widely enjoyed. The color pink and white from cherry blossoms influences food presentation.
*   **Annual Events:** The most famous event is the cherry blossom viewing (sakura blooming). People gather in parks like Yoyama Park in Tokyo or along the Mekong River in Kyoto. Festivals like the "Sakura Festival" are held. Also, the start of the school year and new business cycles often align with spring.

**2. Summer (June～August): heat, humidity, and relaxation**

*   **Lifestyle:** Summer is hot and humid. People focus on cooling down. Air conditioners are used heavily. Fans are common. People wear light, breathable clothes like cotton or linen. Outdoor activities like swimming, beach trips, and camping are popular. However, heatstroke is a concern, so people avoid intense physical activity during the hottest hours.
*   **Food Culture:** Summer foods are refreshing and hydrating. Watermelon, melon, peaches, and grapes are abundant. Cold dishes like chilled tofu, cold noodles (hiyashi chabash), and gazpacho-style soups are common. Ice cream, shaved ice (kakugo), and cold drinks like iced tea and lemonade are essential. Seafood like squid and octopus are grilled on the beach. The concept of "cooling" is central to summer eating.
