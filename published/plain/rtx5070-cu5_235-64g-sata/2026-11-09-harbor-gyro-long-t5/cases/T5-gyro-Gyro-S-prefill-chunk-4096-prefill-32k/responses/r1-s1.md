**Structure Summary**

The records follow a strict, repetitive pattern consisting of two components:

1.  **Key/ID:** A string formatted as `record` followed by a **6-digit zero-padded number** (e.g., `record000001`, `record001721`). The IDs increment sequentially by 1.
2.  **Value:** The constant string `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.
