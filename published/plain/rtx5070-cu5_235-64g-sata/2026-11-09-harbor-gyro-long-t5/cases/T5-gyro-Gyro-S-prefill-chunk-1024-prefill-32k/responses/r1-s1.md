The structure of these records is highly repetitive and uniform. Here is a summary of the structure and how to look up a record by its ID:

### 1. Record Structure
Each record follows a strict, fixed format consisting of two parts:

*   **ID Prefix:** `record` followed by a **6-digit zero-padded integer** (e.g., `record000001`, `record001024`).
*   **Content:** The literal string `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta.`

**Format Pattern:**
`recordXXXXXX: alpha beta gamma delta
