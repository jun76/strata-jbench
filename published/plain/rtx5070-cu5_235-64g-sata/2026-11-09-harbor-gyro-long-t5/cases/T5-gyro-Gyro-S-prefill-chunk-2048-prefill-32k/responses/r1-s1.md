The structure of these records is highly repetitive and uniform. Here is a summary of the structure and how to look up a record by its ID:

### 1. Record Structure
Each record follows a strict format consisting of two parts:
*   **Record ID:** A unique identifier prefixed with `record` followed by a zero-padded 6-digit number (e.g., `record000001`).
*   **Record Content:** The exact same sequence of Greek letters repeated for every single record: `alpha beta gamma delta epsilon zeta eta theta`.
