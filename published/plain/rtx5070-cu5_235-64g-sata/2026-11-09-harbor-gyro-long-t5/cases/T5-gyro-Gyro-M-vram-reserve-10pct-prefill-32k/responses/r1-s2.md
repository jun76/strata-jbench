### 1. Structure of the Records

Each record follows a strict, uniform pattern consisting of two main components:

*   **Identifier (ID):** A prefix in the format `recordXXXXXX`, where `XXXXXX` represents a sequential integer padded with leading zeros to ensure a fixed width of 6 digits (e.g., `record000001`, `record001721`).
*   **Payload (Content):** A fixed string of Greek letters separated by spaces
