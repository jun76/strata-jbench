### 1. Structure of the Records

The records follow a strict, uniform pattern. Each record consists of two main components:

*   **ID (Identifier):** A string in the format `recordNNNNNN`.
    *   `record` is a static prefix.
    *   `NNNNNN` is a zero-padded integer representing the sequential number of the record.
    *   In this dataset, the IDs range from `record000001` to `record000212`.
